## 📐 Raised Bed Soil & Mulch Math

For offline calculations or custom automation scripts:

### 1. Soil Volume Formula
To calculate required soil volume for rectangular raised beds:

$$\text{Cubic Feet} = \text{Length (ft)} \times \text{Width (ft)} \times \left(\frac{\text{Depth (inches)}}{12}\right)$$

### 2. Bulk Delivery Conversion (Cubic Yards)
For bulk soil or compost orders delivered by truck:

$$\text{Cubic Yards} = \frac{\text{Cubic Feet}}{27}$$

### 3. Retail Bag Estimations
Standard store-bought bagged amendments:
* **1.5 cu. ft. bags:** $\text{Bags} = \text{Cubic Feet} \div 1.5$
* **2.0 cu. ft. bags:** $\text{Bags} = \text{Cubic Feet} \div 2.0$
* **Compressed Peat Moss (3.8 cu. ft. bale expands to ~7.6 cu. ft.):** $\text{Bales} = \text{Cubic Feet} \div 7.6$
