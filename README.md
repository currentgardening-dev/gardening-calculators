## 📐 Formulas & Reference Math

### 1. Raised Bed Soil & Mulch Volume Formula
To calculate how much soil or mulch is required:

- **Step 1:** Calculate cubic feet:  
  $$\text{Length (ft)} \times \text{Width (ft)} \times \frac{\text{Depth (inches)}}{12} = \text{Cubic Feet}$$
- **Step 2:** Convert to cubic yards (for bulk orders):  
  $$\text{Cubic Yards} = \frac{\text{Cubic Feet}}{27}$$
- **Step 3:** Convert to standard store bags:  
  - 1.5 cu. ft. bags = $\text{Cubic Feet} \div 1.5$  
  - 2.0 cu. ft. bags = $\text{Cubic Feet} \div 2.0$

### 2. Square-Foot Gardening Spacing Standards
- **1 plant / sq ft:** Tomato, Eggplant, Pepper, Broccoli, Cabbage
- **4 plants / sq ft:** Lettuce, Swiss Chard, Garlic
- **9 plants / sq ft:** Bush Beans, Spinach, Beets
- **16 plants / sq ft:** Carrots, Radishes, Onions
